```text
Use case: stylized-concept / original game character redesign.
The attached sheet is the PREVIOUS DESIGN TO IMPROVE, not a design to preserve literally. The user asks to redo it: a much more MODERN ROBOT CHARACTER with a clearly visible real-looking SCI-FI CANNON, and a genuinely TOP-DOWN gameplay view. Design the character first; DO NOT put it into a game screen yet.
Create ONE landscape 3:2 professional character design sheet with a confident premium sci-fi mobile-game aesthetic on a very clean pale cool-gray studio background, subtle dark-blue section labels, ample spacing.
REDESIGNED CHARACTER:
A compact personable futuristic service/combat robot companion, stout and stable, with a low-profile chamfered hexagonal armored body instead of the previous bulbous toy. Clean cyan armor plates, dark graphite joints/chassis, a little pale titanium trim. A recognizable short stepped antenna at the rear-left with a small amber status light. A TWO-EYE digital visor is built into an UPWARD-FACING dark roof panel/slightly sloped upper deck so BOTH eyes can actually be seen from an overhead gameplay camera. Eyes are expressive short luminous pixel shapes, confident and alert, not angry; no huge baby eyes and no smiley mouth. A few large simple armor facets, compact fixed shoulder panels, two very small foot/skid pads. NO long articulated legs, fingers, dangling accessories or wings. The silhouette remains an unmistakable little ROBOT, not a tank, gun turret alone, flying ship or humanoid soldier.
CRITICAL WEAPON CHANGE:
Give the robot ONE prominent compact energy cannon mounted along its RIGHT shoulder/forearm module. It projects forward well beyond the body, roughly half the body length, with a clearly visible thick rectangular barrel and a dark square muzzle aperture with a cyan inner glow. Short recoil sleeve, one clean power strip, two simple block shapes. The weapon looks like a purposeful sci-fi blaster with a clear direction of fire, NOT a glowing chest button. No realistic firearm diagrams. The cannon is attached securely to a fixed simple arm panel, no detailed hands or finger rig required. It must be visible as a LONG FORWARD-PROJECTING SILHOUETTE in the top view. The visor must remain unobstructed.
SHEET LAYOUT:
LEFT 43 percent: one large beautiful three-quarter perspective CHARACTER CONCEPT view to communicate personality, body construction and weapon. A slightly inquisitive confident pose with the cannon held forward. Shallow stylized game shading, sharp clean shapes, no complex texture.
RIGHT upper 57 percent: one LARGE strict ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP-DOWN VIEW of EXACTLY the same robot, heading NORTH (toward the TOP of the sheet). Camera is exactly 90 degrees above the horizontal floor looking straight DOWN. This is a PLAN VIEW: show roof panels, the roof visor, upper surfaces of shoulder modules and the long cannon extending upward on the viewer's RIGHT. No front face portrait, no tilted isometric view, no horizon, no visible front-facing vertical chest, no fake 'top view' made by slightly tilting a portrait. Body footprint must read clearly as a compact hexagonal block; cannon unmistakably determines NORTH. The chest is NOT visible from overhead. Consistent same colors, same antenna and same weapon.
RIGHT lower: FOUR small consistent STRICT TOP-DOWN direction thumbnails, the same overhead sprite rotated to NORTH, EAST, SOUTH and WEST. These are rotational orientations of the SAME geometry, not different camera angles, never front or side portraits. No ammo numbers on the sprite itself; those belong to the future game interface. Tiny direction arrows below each are acceptable.
Exact Spanish text only: "ROBOT • CONCEPTO", "VISTA CENITAL", "DIRECCIONES". These headings label the three sections. No invented character name, no statistics, no marketing text.
PROGRAMMABILITY: strong simple silhouette, a small number of reusable layered shapes, material-color changes can recolor the armor, no skeletal animation necessary, body/eyes/cannon should be visually separable. Aim for a highly polished design that will still be easy to represent with a 2D sprite plus a small recoil animation. Modern, charismatic and visibly armed with its friendly fictional energy blaster. No game board, no other characters, no logo, no watermark.
```
