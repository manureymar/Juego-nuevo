## 01. Colibrí

```text
Use case: ui-mockup / original pixel-art level illustration.
Edit the supplied FUTURISTIC MOBILE GAME SCREEN. It is the mandatory layout/visual-style reference. The user specifically wants the formation INSIDE the square playfield changed into a gorgeous recognizable figure made from small colored square pixel blocks.
Keep the same orbital station frame, magnetic perimeter track, futuristic angular shooter drones, ammo counters, five waiting bays, three lower launch queues, bottom buttons, camera view, dark blue/cyan environment, portrait 9:16 composition and high-quality finish. Do not redesign the interface or replace the game's genre.
Change the central kite COMPLETELY into the subject below. The entire new illustration MUST be composed of many small UNIFORM SQUARE BLOCKS snapped to ONE regular orthogonal pixel grid, roughly 36 to 44 cells across the motif. Use beautiful deliberate pixel-art cluster shading and a limited harmonious palette. Every contour is stair-stepped square pixels. All blocks have exactly the same scale and shallow depth as the existing game targets. Subtle edge definition between blocks, no thick gaps. Do NOT paint a smooth illustration on top of the blocks. Do NOT use triangles, irregular mosaic tiles, lines or vector curves inside the subject. Do NOT make a 3D animal standing on the board. This is a FLAT PIXEL MOSAIC formed from destructible square game blocks.
Center the whole figure inside the square board and scale it to occupy about 80 percent of the available playfield. Leave a clean dark-grid margin on all sides, with substantial negative space around its silhouette. No part may overlap the track. The pixels must be large enough to read individually on a phone and still richly artistic.
Show the figure INTACT at the start of a display moment: remove the existing flying projectiles and destruction fragments so they do not obscure the art. The three drones may remain on the perimeter but are not firing in this frozen concept frame. Keep their original simple design. The imagery must clearly look like a playable pixel-block formation inside this same futuristic puzzle.
No additional logo, title, animal name, caption or watermark. Only the existing Spanish interface and its numbers. Preserve "EN CINTA 3/5", "ESPERA 2/5", "240".

Replace the level label with exact "NIVEL 01".
SUBJECT: A beautiful hummingbird suspended in flight in side profile, slender stepped beak pointing to the right, uplifted sweeping wing, emerald and turquoise chest, sapphire and violet feathers, a few warm gold highlight squares and a small bright eye. Graceful clear recognizable silhouette, wings and tail formed from skillful staircase pixel clusters, a coherent connected mosaic. Lush jewel colors and careful pixel shading, neither a flat generic bird icon nor a realistic feather painting.

```
